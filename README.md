# NewPoint ERP System Analysis

This group project focuses on the systems analysis and design of the NewPoint ERP system for Al-Nahdah Auto Parts Company, with the goal of improving business operations, streamlining processes, and supporting better decision-making through a more efficient and integrated information system.

The project was developed as part of KAU MIS377: System Analysis and Design.

---

## Overview

NewPoint ERP is used to support core business activities such as inventory management, purchasing, supplier coordination, and operational reporting. However, as business needs evolve, the existing system may face limitations in efficiency, visibility, and process integration.

This project analyzes the current system, identifies weaknesses and operational gaps, and proposes a redesigned solution that enhances productivity, improves data accuracy, and supports more effective management decisions.

---

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to evaluate the current ERP system and design a more effective solution for Al-Nahdah Auto Parts Company by:

- identifying operational problems in the current processes
- analyzing business requirements through stakeholder input and documentation
- modeling existing and proposed business workflows
- improving inventory, purchasing, and supplier management processes
- enhancing reporting and decision support capabilities
- designing a future-state system aligned with organizational needs

---

## Key Business Challenges Addressed

- Inefficient inventory monitoring and replenishment processes
- Limited visibility into supplier performance and quotations
- Delayed purchasing and order tracking
- Inconsistent reporting and weak analytical support
- Difficulty managing stock levels and supplier response times
- Lack of streamlined workflows across departments

---

## Proposed System Improvements

The redesigned ERP solution introduces several improvements, including:

- Automated stock alerts and reorder notifications
- Supplier comparison dashboard for quotations and offers
- Better tracking of purchase orders and deliveries
- End-to-end purchasing workflow monitoring
- Analytical dashboards for inventory and supplier performance
- Improved reporting for management and operational teams

---

## System Analysis and Design Scope

This project includes a structured systems analysis of the organization’s current processes and requirements, covering:

- Requirements gathering through interviews, surveys, and business documents
- As-Is process analysis
- To-Be system design
- Context diagrams
- Data flow diagrams (DFD Level 0 and Level 1)
- Functional and non-functional requirements
- Agile planning using MoSCoW prioritization
- Sprint-based project execution
